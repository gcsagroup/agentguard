# Launch-support runbook

Each of six deliverables needs a public support entry point, timezone/on-call schedule, owner, and escalation chain, consistent across store, in-app, and privacy links. Prepare reviewed responses for signing/install, permissions, observation, false positive/negative, confirmation queue, audit recovery, and security reporting. Collect only necessary version, channel, error code, and redacted logs—never passwords, signing keys, tokens, full screens, or unrelated files.

A ticket records receipt time, platform/version/channel, symptom, impact, and consented diagnostics. Classify usage, known limitation, defect, security/privacy incident, or store/signing failure. Anything affecting protection semantics, multiple users, update chain, or personal data escalates immediately to [incident response](incident-response.en.md); support does not downgrade it. Responses state only verified facts, recoverable steps, and a real next-update time.

Aggregate volume, response/resolution time, repeats, severity, and opt-outs by channel. Operations includes the support summary in every Beta and rollout-stage health decision. Support-document fixes are reviewed, versioned, and recorded.
